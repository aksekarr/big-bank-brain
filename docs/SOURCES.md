# Phase 0: Source-feasibility log

Candidate scope and selection criteria are defined in `SPEC.md`, section 4.1.
Assess credibility, analytical quality, publishing frequency, technical accessibility
and acceptable usage terms before selecting the MVP's 4-5 sources.
Only public text articles are in scope; existing access and reuse rules still apply.

Avi approved BBVA Research, ABN AMRO Group Economics, New York Fed / Liberty Street
Economics and BIS on 2026-09-10. Their dated approval notes below supersede earlier
pending-selection/fallback wording, without removing the recorded caveats. Bank of
America Institute and the other listed candidates remain unapproved. Technical
discovery findings for five candidates were recorded on 2026-09-10; other assessments
remain pending. **To investigate** means
the fact has not yet been established, including institution classifications.
When researching, record supporting URLs, review dates and evidence in the relevant
cells or notes. Publishing frequency should record text articles in the last 14 days.

Overall statuses:

- **Candidate**: awaiting or undergoing investigation; not approved for collection.
- **Approved**: Avi has reviewed the website terms and robots.txt and approved the source
  under the specification's source gates.
- **Excluded**: not selected for use; record the reason and supporting evidence in notes.

Central banks and major multilateral financial/economic institutions are fallback
candidates only. Their investigation does not redefine the primary editorial proposition;
that remains a product decision for Avi. Fallback labels below record investigation
scope, not findings about suitability or access.

Technical discovery ratings in Notes are separate from approval: **Easy** = a working
structured feed with useful metadata; **Workable** = a usable public listing with parsing
or coverage limitations; **Difficult** = substantial unresolved discovery obstacles;
**Blocked** = access controls or robots rules prevent the assessed route.

The 2026-09-10 checks used official public pages/endpoints and ordinary HTTP GETs with
`BigBankBrain/0.1 (source-feasibility research)` as the User-Agent. All five starting
pages and robots files returned HTTP 200. Some sandboxed requests failed DNS resolution;
the same checks succeeded after permission to run outside the sandbox. The web research
tool also failed internally on a Bank of America category link; this was not an origin
HTTP verdict. No access controls were bypassed. No article pages or PDF bodies were
fetched, and no raw responses were saved. The Liberty Street Economics RSS response
includes a full-content field; only discovery metadata was inspected, and the response
was discarded. No collector was built. These are point-in-time local checks, not a
scheduled reliability test or the GitHub Actions runner test required later by the spec.
RSS absence is not proven by failure to find an advertised feed. Terms/reuse, publishing
frequency over 14 days, and final approval remain **To investigate**.
