    HOW BIG BANK BRAIN WORKS

    A deliberately bounded route from public research to a reviewable briefing.

    1. 01Public research

      Only approved, public institutional analysis enters the process.

    2. 02AI extraction

      Research is converted into discrete, attributable claims.

    3. 03Structured evidence

      Every claim retains its article and institution reference.

    4. 04AI synthesis

      The briefing groups supported evidence into an editorial read.

    5. 05Factual verification

      An independent guardrail checks support before review.

    6. 06Human review

      A person makes the final decision before anything is presented as reviewed.

    A SMALL BUT IMPORTANT TEST

    Words can smuggle in assumptions.

    In a controlled ABN AMRO evaluation, the phrase “Republican Senate” was changed to “Republicans retaining the Senate.” The earlier verifier missed the added implication that Republicans already held the Senate. A presupposition-aware verifier caught it, demonstrating why wording—not just topic overlap—matters when checking evidence.
