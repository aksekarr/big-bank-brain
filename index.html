    HOW BIG BANK BRAIN WORKS

    A deliberately bounded route from public research to a reviewable briefing.

    1. 01Public research

      Approved institutions publish the source material; BBB only links back to it.

    2. 02AI extraction

      AI turns permitted research into specific claims with source references.

    3. 03Structured claims

      The claims are kept as evidence that can be inspected point by point.

    4. 04AI synthesis

      AI writes a concise briefing using that bounded evidence set.

    5. 05Verification

      An independent factual guardrail checks whether the evidence supports it.

    6. 06Human review

      A human decides whether the briefing is ready to be marked reviewed.

    BBB’s read is checked to make sure every statement points to real extracted claims. It isn’t run through the factual verifier.

    A SMALL BUT IMPORTANT TEST

    Words can smuggle in assumptions.

    In a controlled ABN AMRO evaluation, the phrase “Republican Senate” was changed to “Republicans retaining the Senate.” The earlier verifier missed the added implication that Republicans already held the Senate. A presupposition-aware verifier caught it, demonstrating why wording—not just topic overlap—matters when checking evidence.
